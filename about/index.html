About me

How RIT, studying abroad, projects, and leadership have shaped what I want to build next.

I came to RIT interested in how business and technology work together. Since then, coursework, travel, research, and student organizations have helped me connect systems thinking with the people those systems serve.

Starting at RIT

Finding the intersection

At Saunders College of Business, I chose Management Information Systems because it brings together technical problem-solving and organizational decisions. My minor in AI in Society and Environmental Studies immersion have pushed me to ask broader questions about how technology affects communities and the world around us.

Spring 2025 · Zagreb, Croatia

Learning beyond campus

Studying at RIT Croatia gave me the chance to learn in a different setting, work with people across cultures, and explore more of Europe. In a collaboration with Oakland University students, I analyzed employee retention and engagement at Microsoft, bringing cross-cultural perspectives into our recommendations.

Summer 2026 · Victor, NY

Putting research into practice

The internship connected my interests in data, business, and technology to everyday decisions about outreach and business development.
