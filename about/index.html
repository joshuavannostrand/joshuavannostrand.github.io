Beyond the résumé

About me

A little about how I got here, what I’ve been involved in, and where I want to go next.

My time at RIT has been about more than my major. Studying abroad, joining student organizations, doing research, and working in healthcare vendor finance have all helped me get a clearer sense of the work I want to do—and given me a lot of experiences I’m glad to have had.

Before RIT

Finishing high school

High school graduation marked the end of one chapter and the start of figuring out what I wanted to do next. That next step brought me to RIT.

Starting at RIT

Finding my direction

I chose Management Information Systems at Saunders because I wanted to understand both the technology and the organizations using it. Over time, I’ve become especially interested in how data and better systems can help in healthcare and environmental industries.

My AI in Society minor and Environmental Studies immersion give me a chance to think about the people and issues behind the technology, alongside the technical work.

April 2024

Joining Theta Chi

I was initiated into Theta Chi in April 2024. It’s become an important part of my time at RIT, and I now serve as Recruitment Chairman, helping plan events, coordinate outreach, and welcome prospective members.

Spring 2025 · Zagreb, Croatia

A semester abroad

Studying at RIT Croatia meant living and learning somewhere new, meeting people from different backgrounds, and traveling around Europe. Cinque Terre was one of the places I got to visit along the way.

I also worked with students from Oakland University on an employee retention and engagement analysis focused on Microsoft. It was a chance to collaborate across cultures as well as explore outside the classroom.

Fall 2025

Sharing my experience abroad

Back at RIT, I presented about my time abroad and shared advice with students considering it themselves. It was a chance to talk through what the experience was like and help others think about taking that step.

2025–2026

Getting more hands-on

Through research on AI in hospitality and tourism, I’ve been learning how new tools can affect day-to-day operations and the people doing the work. My class projects have given me room to try different approaches, from building business software to predicting customer churn and analyzing energy data.

The energy project especially connects with the kind of work I want to keep doing: using data to understand a problem and help people make more informed decisions.

March 2026 · Atlanta, Georgia

Traveling with the Honors Program

I joined an Honors Program trip to Atlanta in March. The visits and time with other students became another memorable part of my RIT experience beyond campus.

March 2026

Joining Beta Gamma Sigma

I was inducted into Beta Gamma Sigma, the International Business Honors Society. It’s a milestone I’m proud of as I continue working toward my degree at Saunders.

2026 · Campus leadership

Staying involved at RIT

As Director of the Peer Mentorship Program at the Leadership Academy @ Saunders, I help match mentors and mentees and coordinate training and events. I want students to have people they can turn to as they find their way at RIT.

I’m also Webmaster for Business Technology Professionals after serving as President and Vice President. I help keep more than 350 members connected with information, events, and opportunities to learn.

Summer 2026 · Victor, NY

My internship at First American

As a Sales Support Intern in Vendor Finance at First American Equipment Finance, I reviewed a report containing 1,000+ equipment manufacturers and software developers to identify prospective partners. I researched selected companies and built profiles to support the Healthcare team’s outreach.

That experience gave me a closer look at businesses serving healthcare and how useful, well-organized information supports everyday decisions. It also reinforced my interest in working with technology and data in industries that affect people’s lives.

Looking ahead · May 2027 graduation

What I want to do next

I want to work in tech and data, building systems that make it easier for people and organizations to do their work well. I’m especially interested in healthcare and environmental industries, where better information and better technology can help address problems that matter to me.

I’m still learning which role will be the right fit, but that’s the direction I want to keep moving in.
