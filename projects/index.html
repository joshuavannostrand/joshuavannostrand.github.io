Machine learning · 2026

Customer Churn Prediction

Overview

Engineered 13 features from 2,520 customer records, compared classification models, and reached 94.4% cross validation accuracy.

What I did

Compared Random Forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM models. Examined classification performance alongside the financial cost of errors, then identified inactivity and customer complaints as key churn signals.

Result

Reached 94.4% cross-validation accuracy and identified account inactivity and customer complaints as leading churn-risk signals.

PythonJupyterLabScikit-learn
